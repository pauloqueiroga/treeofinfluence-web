# Contributing

Thank you for sharing what you learned. The Contributing page on the Tree of Influence website explains the details. In short:

- Experience reports, ideas, questions, and things to show go in **Discussions**. Each category has a short form.
- Corrections to the website go in **Issues**, with the correction form.
- Remove the names of companies, products, teams, and people before you post. Everything here is public.
- Ask participants before sharing a photo of their board.
- Be kind. Critique ideas, not people.

Contributions are published under CC BY 4.0, the same license as the website. You keep the copyright to what you write.
