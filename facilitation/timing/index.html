Timing templates

How long each step takes for small, medium, and large groups.

This page gives a starting agenda for each group size. The step pages explain what happens in each step; the times here add them up.

How long each step takes may change with group size. Even in a small, fast group, every step happens, however briefly.

Small group: 3 to 7 participants

About 60 to 90 minutes.

Step Time Notes
Prepare 15-30 min, before Facilitator only
Seed 10-20 min High energy, fast contributions
Group 5-10 min Small boards de-duplicate fast
Connect 15-25 min Takes longer than it looks
Prioritize 10-15 min Dot voting or verbal agreement
Decompose 20-30 min 1 or 2 deep dives, as one group
Close 5-10 min Read back, save, agree on next steps

A first session with a group new to the technique usually skips Decompose and fits in 60 to 90 minutes. See the Quick Start.

Medium group: 8 to 15 participants

About 2 to 2.5 hours.

Step Time Notes
Prepare Before the session Facilitator only
Seed 20-30 min More nodes; allow extra time
Group 10-15 min More duplicates and some merge decisions
Connect 20-30 min Pairs may draw a first pass of arrows
Break 10 min Between Connect and Prioritize
Prioritize 15-20 min Leave time to discuss the results
Decompose 30-40 min 2 or 3 deep dives, subgroups of 3 or 4 in parallel
Close 5-10 min Don't sacrifice this step if running out of time

Large group: 16 to 30 participants

About 2.5 to 3.5 hours.

Step Time Notes
Prepare Before the session Pre-seeding matters most at this size
Seed 30-40 min Expect 50 to 80 nodes or more
Group 15-20 min Small groups each cluster one area
Connect 30-40 min The full group for major arrows, then subgroups for their areas
Break 15 min Required at this size
Prioritize 20-30 min Dot voting, then discussion of the top results
Decompose 45-60 min 3 to 5 subgroups in parallel
Close 5-10 min Don't sacrifice this step if running out of time

If energy is a concern, split the session in 2, as for groups of more than 30.

More than 30 participants

Split the work across 2 or more sessions. In the first, you may run Seed and Group. Between sessions, the facilitator tidies the board: stacks remaining duplicates, labels clusters, and checks the parking lot. In the second, run Connect and Prioritize. Decompose may happen in subgroups afterwards. See planning a session.

Adjusting on the day

  • Read the room. A highly aligned group can compress time, but a group with deep disagreements needs more time overall.
  • Add buffer for transitions. Moving from one step to the next takes a few minutes each time.
  • When the session runs late, shorten Decompose, not Close.

Example

The renewals session had 12 participants and followed the medium template:

Step Planned Actual
Seed 20-30 min 25 min
Group 10-15 min 15 min
Connect 20-30 min 30 min
Break 10 min 10 min
Prioritize 15-20 min 20 min
Decompose 30-40 min 35 min
Close 5-10 min 10 min

Connect ran to its limit because of the loop the group had to untangle. Decompose was too short for the engineering subgroup, so the outages node got its own deep-dive session a week later.
