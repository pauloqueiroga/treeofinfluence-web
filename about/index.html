About

Where the Tree of Influence came from, how it compares to other frameworks, and how you may use this material.

The Tree of Influence was not designed in a lab. It grew out of real workshops, got its name from a joke, and has been refined through use since then.

The text and diagrams on this website are free to reuse under CC BY 4.0. Everyone who uses the technique is invited to contribute what they learn.
